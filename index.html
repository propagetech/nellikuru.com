What we do

Practical metallurgy that pays back on every heat

We are an R&D startup focused on iron and steel making in India. We turn proven chemistry into simple, shop-floor-ready reagents that lower silicon and protect yield, without a separate process step or heavy capital.

Lower silicon

Reduce hot metal silicon in the blast furnace main runner and in hot metal ladles, for more consistent steel making.

Higher yield

Insulate the metal from the moment it enters the ladle to cut temperature loss, oxidation and jamming.

User-friendly

No separate steel-making step and no oxygen blowing. Our reagents are added in the runner or at the bottom of the ladle.

R&D-driven

Built by inventors under the Make in India program, with the Nellikuru Process under patent.

Our solutions

Two innovations for the cast house

Both work with your existing flow, in the runner and the ladle, with no new process line.

Hot Metal Desiliconization

The patented Nellikuru Process lowers hot metal silicon in the blast furnace or COREX cast house, in the runner or the ladle. Low silicon is ideal for steel making.

Read more

Hot Metal Insulation Compound

Added at the bottom of the ladle before tapping, it forms an insulation layer the instant it meets liquid metal, floats with it, and stays intact until the metal is poured.
