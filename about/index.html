About us

Inventors for the iron and steel industry

Nellikuru Innovations is the first startup of its kind in research and development for iron and steel making in India, under the Make in India program.

Our aim is to encourage true inventors in this field to commercialize their innovation for the benefit of industry and mankind. We focus on practical metallurgy: ideas that work on the shop floor, not just in the lab.

Today we supply two innovative solutions to the iron and steel industries: hot metal desiliconization in the blast furnace or COREX cast house, in the runner or the ladle, and a hot metal insulation compound that improves yield.

How we think

Proven chemistry, made easy to use

Steelmakers want consistency without complexity. Everything we build is shaped around that.

Practical

Solutions that fit your existing cast house, with no separate process line and no oxygen blowing.

Consistent

Aimed at giving steel making the steady hot metal quality it depends on, heat after heat.

Original

Home-grown innovation under the Make in India program, with the Nellikuru Process under patent.
